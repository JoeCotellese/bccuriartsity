A Danish Modern bookcase cabinet by Johannes Sorth, with three open shelves
over a book-matched back panel and a lower cabinet behind two sliding doors.
It has Sorth's flared side profile and richly figured wood, and at just over
six feet tall it gives a room real height without taking up much floor.
