An original, signed oil on board by W.T. Carlsen, a Danish-born painter who
moved to Canada in 1954 and later to the U.S. A tall, narrow cityscape, most
likely Montreal, in his signature cream, red, and blue, set in a metallic frame
that makes the painting seem to float.
