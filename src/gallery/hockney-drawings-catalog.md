A 1996 first-edition catalog from the LACMA retrospective of David Hockney's
drawings, published by Chronicle Books — 165 works spanning four decades,
from his California pools to his travels through Egypt and Morocco. Scholarly
essays inside, but it holds its own as a coffee-table piece for anyone who
loves mid-century and Pop Art.
