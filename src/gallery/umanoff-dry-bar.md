A 1950s dry bar by Arthur Umanoff, mixing wrought iron, wood slats studded
with rows of small nail heads, and tightly wrapped rush panels. A wide top
overhangs an open frame with shelves on the bartender's side, all on a slim
black iron base with a built-in footrail. At 38 inches wide, it fits a living
room or den without needing a whole bar.
