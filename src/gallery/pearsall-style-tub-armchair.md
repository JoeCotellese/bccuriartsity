A midcentury-style tub chair in the manner of Adrian Pearsall, with a
wraparound back that sweeps forward into angled, winged arms. Upholstered in
oatmeal tweed on slim, tapered dark wood legs. Compact enough to work as a
reading chair, a desk chair, or the host chair at the end of the table.
