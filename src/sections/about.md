I believe that interesting people thrive in interesting spaces.

Mass-market art and décor can be just fine. I've certainly bought plenty of it and liked it well enough. But the more time goes by, the more I want to create a home that feels eclectic and interesting and totally unexpected, just like me. I'm pretty unique.

I bet you are, too.

I hunt for the pieces that feel rare and thoughtful — objects with a real maker's hand behind them. I'm drawn to work that has texture, personality, and won't apologize for being strange. And in today's world of AI everything, I'm even more drawn to things that were created for humans, by humans.

We all contain multitudes. Your space should, too.

## About me

<img src="/images/trish-headshot.jpg" alt="Trish Sammer" class="headshot">

I'm Trish Sammer, a lifelong writer, seeker, and collector of the uncommon. I've spent the past few decades making my living as a journalist and editor. In 2021, I went looking for one painting and came home with a habit. Enough people told me I had "an eye" that I eventually started reselling my found treasures, first online and now in person at Umbrella Antiques & Modern in Hopewell, NJ.
